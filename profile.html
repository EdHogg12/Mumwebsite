About the Artist

Jess Wallwork

Jessa studied Fine Art at Edinburgh, a combined degree in Art History at Edinburgh University and Fine Art at Edinburgh College of Art. At this time Jessa began studying fresco painting, enjoying the way the process informs the subject. Jessa’ wall paintings reflect this learning. Her work plays with the flat surface, the concept of space, and the changing natural light in the room. At times stylised, at other times naturalistic, the flora is drawn from life, with frequent reference to art history. Before beginning a commission, Jessa likes to consider the significance of a particular time of year, the flowers that are about to bloom or in the process of dying at that time. A particular time of day can also play a part. Jessa embraces imperfection and transience. Her large and small wall paintings create architectural elements that enhance the life of a room. “I use natural pigments to paint. Each project is a unique collaboration with the space and its inhabitants. The ideas come from a process of listening. I love to create contemplative and lasting impression in the room."
